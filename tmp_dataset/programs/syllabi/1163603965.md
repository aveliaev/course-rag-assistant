#### Аннотация

The course develops the English language skills that Master’s students in Big Data Analytics need to participate effectively in academic and professional communication in Data Science. It combines elements of General English, Business English, and English for Academic Purposes. It is organized around realistic communicative tasks: professional self-introduction, discussing data and analytical results, participating in online meetings, delivering presentations, discussing research materials, and taking part in job interviews. Particular attention is given to pronunciation, word and sentence stress, rhythm and intonation, as well as to the accurate and fluent production of numbers, formulas, and metrics. Students work with authentic professional and academic materials and practice structuring spoken responses, justifying decisions, asking clarifying questions, discussing limitations, and responding to feedback.

#### Цель освоения дисциплины

- The course aims to develop practical communicative competence in English for academic and professional Data Science contexts, with particular attention to clear spoken communication, professional vocabulary, functional grammar, pronunciation, data commentary, presentations, teamwork and workplace interaction.

#### Планируемые результаты обучения

- give a concise and appropriate professional self-introduction and participate in small talk.
- use core grammar accurately in professional and academic communication, especially tense/aspect, modality, conditionals and passive structures.
- use Data Science and workplace vocabulary, collocations and functional expressions appropriately.
- understand the main ideas and relevant details in professional videos, short research-oriented texts and technical communication materials.
- describe charts, graphs, tables, metrics and numerical information clearly and accurately.
- distinguish direct observation from interpretation, assumption and evidence when talking about data.
- deliver an organised spoken explanation using signposting, logical sequencing and appropriate transitions.
- communicate in online meetings, discuss problems, compare options, negotiate decisions and recommend next steps.
- participate in presentations and Q&A, ask for clarification, answer questions and provide constructive feedback.
- communicate their experience and skills in a job-interview setting.
- deliver an independent Data Commentary based on a visualisation, using appropriate language for trends, comparison, interpretation and conclusion.
- speak with sufficient fluency and intelligibility for academic and professional communication, using stress, rhythm and intonation to support meaning.
