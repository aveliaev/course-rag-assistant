#### Abstract

The course outlines the main methods and models of system analysis inconditions of certainty, and under various types of uncertainty of:-input, associated with the interaction of people and organizations pursuing different goals;-modeling system determined by the nature of its functioning in a complex and unstable environment;-goal associated with the presence of several criteria for assessing the response of the system to control actions or with the absence of formal criteria for such an assessment.The course contains a large number of learning tasks, as well as a significant number of applied examples and real cases.
