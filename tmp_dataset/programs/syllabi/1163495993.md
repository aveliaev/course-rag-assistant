#### Abstract

AI literacy is one of the digital competencies embedded in all HSE University degree programmes. It concerns the use of digital technologies and information-handling tools to meet personal, educational and professional needs and to support collaboration in digital environments, with due regard for fundamental security principles and ethical and legal standards. All first-year undergraduate students take an independent assessment of AI Literacy to demonstrate that they have attained this competency. The independent AI examination is administered using proctoring procedures and lasts 60 minutes. It comprises a test component and a practical component. The final result is converted to a 1-10 scale. Scores below 4 are rounded down by discarding the decimal part; scores of 4 or above are rounded to the nearest whole number. Failure to obtain a passing result in the Independent AI Literacy Assessment within the prescribed timeframe will be treated as a failure to fulfil the programme requirements.

#### Learning Objectives

- To assess students' readiness to undertake advanced professionally oriented courses; their ability to search for information and data, interpret and create instructions, interact with others in digital environments, and protect their digital identity.

#### Expected Learning Outcomes

- Searches for, aggregates, analyzes, selects, and structures relevant information, verifies its reliability, and uses it appropriately; Follows existing instructions, analyzes them, and creates new instructions or corrects errors in existing ones; Interacts with others safely and ethically using digital technologies; Protects their digital identity, personal digital space, and digital devices; Uses and maintains digital devices and technologies.
- Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to: search for, aggregate, analyse, select and structure relevant information, verify its reliability, and use it appropriately; follow and analyse existing instructions, create new instructions, and correct errors in existing instructions; interact with others safely and ethically using digital technologies; protect their digital identity, digital environment and digital devices; use and maintain digital devices and technologies.
