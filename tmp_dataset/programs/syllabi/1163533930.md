#### Abstract

Information security risks are potential possibilities for threats to exploit vulnerabilities in an asset or group of assets, leading to harm and potential losses for an organization. Managing information security risks involves the timely identification, assessment, and mitigation of these risks. This course covers current standards in risk management, relevant methodologies, and real-world practical scenarios. Student knowledge is assessed through seminar participation, homework assignments, practical exercises, and the final exam.

#### Learning Objectives

- Provides the knowledge and practical skills necessary to effectively manage information security risks within an organization

#### Expected Learning Outcomes

- understand core concepts of risk management approach to Information Security
- apply Russian and international information security standards
- use risk assessment models for information systems
- develop risk mitigation strategies and analyze real-world scenarios
- enhance decision-making skills based on risk analysis

#### Course Contents
